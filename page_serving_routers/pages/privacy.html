Last updated: February 2026  ·  Your privacy is our priority

We collect only what we need to deliver the service. We do not sell your data. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

01

Who We Are

WANDERWITHAI ("we", "our", "us") operates the platform available at wanderwithai.com. We are responsible for the personal data you provide to us when using our service.

For privacy-related queries, contact us at privacy@wanderwithai.com.

02

Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to provide our service:

Data Type Purpose Retention
Email address Account creation, login, communications Until account deletion
Hashed password Authentication (never stored in plain text) Until account deletion
YouTube URLs submitted Itinerary generation Duration of account
Generated itineraries Access and re-download your plans Duration of account
Credit balance & transaction history Billing and dispute resolution 7 years (legal requirement)
IP address & browser info Security, fraud prevention 90 days

03

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the WANDERWITHAI platform
  • Process payments and manage your credit balance
  • Send transactional emails (account verification, payment receipts)
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

04

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third parties to operate our platform:

  • Razorpay Payment processing. Your card/UPI data is handled entirely by Razorpay and is never stored on our servers.
  • Google Gemini API AI processing of travel data. YouTube video content is processed to generate itineraries.
  • MongoDB Atlas Secure cloud database hosting for your account and itinerary data.

Each third party operates under their own privacy policy and applicable data protection laws.

05

Cookies

We use minimal, essential cookies only:

  • Authentication tokens Stored as HTTP-only cookies to keep you logged in securely.
  • Session data Temporary session identifiers that expire when you close your browser.

We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies.

06

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage
  • All data in transit is encrypted via TLS/HTTPS
  • Authentication uses short-lived JWT tokens
  • Database access is restricted to authorised services only

07

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Portability Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection Object to specific uses of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@wanderwithai.com. We will respond within 30 days.

08

Children's Privacy

WANDERWITHAI is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a prominent notice on the platform. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.
